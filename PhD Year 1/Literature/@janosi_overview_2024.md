Paper which focuses on the finite-time dynamics of non-autonomous choatic systems of the form:

$$
\dot x = f(x, t, u(t))
$$
where direct dependence on $t$ is known to be periodic. 

**Concepts Discussed:**
Snapshot Attactor 
Instaneous Lyapunov Exponents.
Natural Measure 
Comparsion to frozen dynamics 


**Difference from Standard System:**

The classic paper of https://www.scopus.com/pages/publications/35949018382 details ergodic properties of chaotic attractors. These results do not apply as they the parameter drift breaks ergodicity. 

Generally cannot consider the asymptotic case. 

Two general approaches to thinking abotu 

**Lyapunov Exponents: **

The author offer a generalised notion of the Lyapunov exponents to non-autonomous system. 

This measure of based on the [[Ensemble Averaged Pairwise Distance (EAPD)]].

In an ergodic system, ensemble and time averages are equivalent. However, the introduction of the parameter drift means the system is no longer ergodic and it does not make sense to take time limits. However, taking the instaneuous rate of change of an ensemble would allow for a measure of exponential growth for a fixed $u(t)$. 

($u(t)$ not bounded to time limit doesn't make sense).
