
Let $f: \mathcal{X} \to \mathcal{X}$ and $g: \mathcal{Y} \to \mathcal{Y}$ be two continuous functions. 

The functions are said to be *topologically conjugate* if there exists a  function $h: \mathcal{X} \to \mathcal{Y}$.  continuous bijection with a continuous inverse (homeomorphism). 

$h$ is called the conjugacy between $f$ and $g$. 
$$
f = h^{-1}\circ g \ \circ h
$$

Properties Preserved:

Periodic points, Topological entropy, symbolic dynamics, transitive orbits and chaos/not chaos.

Every unimodal map is conjugate to the logisitic map. 

Global conjugacies are uncommon, Hartman-Grobman theorem proves global conjugacies. 

Hartman-Grobman Theorem can be extended to entire basin of attraction if all eigenvalues lie inside the unit circle.
