# Neural ODE's

In Tegelen et al (2025) Neural ODE's are trained with a static bifurcation parameter. Ability to extrapolate past bifurcation points in the Rossler and Lorenz systems was shown. Additionally the ability to learn a global bifurcation in the Lokta-volterra model [[@tegelen_neural_2025]].
