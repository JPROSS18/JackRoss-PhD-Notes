**Definition 4.7 (Hyperbolicity).**

A matrix $A \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times d}$ is called **hyperbolic** if all eigenvalues 
$\lambda$ of $A$ have non‑zero real part, i.e.
$$
\Re(\lambda) \neq 0.
$$


An equilibrium $x^*$ of the differential equation
$$
\dot{x} = f(x),
$$
where $f : D \subset \mathbb{R}^d \to \mathbb{R}^d$ is continuously differentiable, is called **hyperbolic** if the Jacobian $f'(x^*) \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times d}$ is hyperbolic.
