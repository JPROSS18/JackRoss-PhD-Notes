There are quite a number of excellent books on approximation theory. Three
classics are [Cheney 1966], [Davis 1975], and [Meinardus 1967], and a slightly
more recent computationally oriented classic is [Powell 1981]. Perhaps the first
approximation theory text was [Borel 1905].

Chebyshev Polynomials are generally to best approximations on an interval and difference between best Chebyshev Polynomial and 'best' polynomial is negligible. 

Key problems of approximation theory/numerical analysis are: 
- interpolation
- quadrature
- rootfinding
- analytic continuation
- extrapolation of sequences and series 
- solution to differential equations

sequences and series, and solution of diﬀerential equations.


Approximation theory is a well-established branch of mathematics. It can be generally divided into four distinct eras. 
- Chebyshev 
- Classical 
- Neoclassical 
- Numerical
