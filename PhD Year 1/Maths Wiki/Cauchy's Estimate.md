Let $f$ be a holomorphic function on the open ball $B(a, r)$ in $\mathbb C$. 

If $|f|$ is bounded by $M$ over $B(a, r)$, then $\forall n >0$
$$
|f^{(n)}| \leq \frac{n!}{r^n}M 
$$

**Notes:**
An [[entire ]] function is a function which holomorphic on the entire complex plane. 
Liouville's theorem states that a bounded entire function must be constant.
