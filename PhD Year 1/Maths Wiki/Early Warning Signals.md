
# Tipping in Dynamical Systems

[[Tipping in Dynamical Systems]]

Nonlinear systems subjected to a slow varying input parameter (external forcing) can respond to forcing with abrupt behaviours even for arbitrarily slow forcing.

It is important to attempt to forecast upcoming tipping points. 

Main method of doing so it measuring CSD.

# Important Literature


**CSD:**

[Identifying best-indicator species for abrupt transitions in multispecies communities - Dakos 2018](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1470160X17306611?via%3Dihub)

Discussion of EWS in ecological tipping points. 


Early warning signals for critical transitions in complex systems - George et al (2023):](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/1402-4896/acde20)

Review paper of EWS (mostly CSD based). 

**Predicting Slowing Down:**
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-39810-w#change-history

**Limitations of EWS:**
[Uncertainties too large to predict tipping times of major Earth system components from historical data - Ben-Yami et al (2024)](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.adl4841)
Argues that EWS for climate tipping are not good enough to accurately predict tipping. 

[Built for Stability - P.Valdes 2011](https://www.nature.com/articles/ngeo1200) argues existing climate models are too stable as they fail to model past climate tipping events. 

[Ambiguity of early warning signals for climate tipping points - Rietkerk et al 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41558-025-02328-8) argues loss of resilience which CSD based methods tracks does not always indicate a proximity to a tipping point. 


**Predicting Tipping:**
[Warning of a forthcoming collapse of the Atlantic meridional overturning circulation - Ditlevsen & Ditlevsen ](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-39810-w#MOESM1) - Continues trends in statistics which track a loss of stability in real data to make a prediction of AMOC collapse. 








# Skill of an Early Warning Signal

The idea of the 'skill' of an early warning signal was proposed in: 
[Early warning skill, extrapolation and tipping for accelerating cascades - Ashwin et al](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01981)

Use ROC curve methods to quantify usefulness and accuracy of EWS for predicting future tipping.
