# Regression for fitting normal form:
What to fit the model: 
$$
\frac{dx}{dt} = (x -a)^2 + r_0 + r_1 t 
$$
$$
= x^2 - 2ax + a^2 +r_0 + r_1t
$$


to the trajectory 
$$
\{ (t_i,  x_i) \}_{0 \leq i < N}
$$
of $N+1$ equally spaced data points by regression: 


$$
\left [    

 \begin{matrix} 
 
 x_1  \\ 
 
 \vdots  \\
  \vdots   \\
 x_{N} 

 
 \end{matrix} 
\right]

= 
\left [    

 \begin{matrix} 
 
 x_0 & \int^{t_{i+1}}_{t_i} x(s)^2 ds & \int^{t_{i+1}}_{t_i} x(s) ds & \Delta t & \frac{(\Delta t)^2}{2} + t_{0}\Delta t  \\ 
 
 \vdots & & & & \vdots \\
  \vdots & & & & \vdots \\
 x_{N-1} & \int^{t_{i+1}}_{t_i} x(s)^2 ds & \int^{t_{i+1}}_{t_i} x(s) ds & \Delta t & \frac{(\Delta t)^2}{2} + t_{N-1} \Delta  t
 
 \end{matrix} 
\right]

\left[ 
\begin{matrix}
1 \\
1 \\
-2a \\
a^2 + r_0 \\
r_1

\end{matrix}
\right]
$$
which we will write as: 

$$
Y = X\beta
$$
$$
\beta_{fit} = \underset{\beta}{\operatorname{argmin}} || Y - X \beta ||
$$


Goal is to find optimal: $(a, r_0, r_1)$

$$

\left[
\begin{matrix}
(x_0, t_0) \\
\vdots\\
\vdots \\
(x_{N-1}, t_{N-1})
\end{matrix}

\right] \to
\left [    

 \begin{matrix} 
 
 x_0 & \int^{t_{i+1}}_{t_i} x(s)^2 ds & \int^{t_{i+1}}_{t_i} x(s) ds & \Delta t & \frac{(\Delta t)^2}{2} + t_{0}\Delta t  \\ 
 
 \vdots & & & & \vdots \\
  \vdots & & & & \vdots \\
 x_{N-1} & \int^{t_{i+1}}_{t_i} x(s)^2 ds & \int^{t_{i+1}}_{t_i} x(s) ds & \Delta t & \frac{(\Delta t)^2}{2} + t_{N-1} \Delta  t
 
 \end{matrix} 
\right]

$$
